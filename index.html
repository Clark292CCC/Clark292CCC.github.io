Goonin

Buffet Reviews

Goonin is a free app for scoring Chinese buffets and their crab rangoons. Every buffet shows its Yelp rating, the Mullet Score, a Buffet Score (food, variety, cleanliness, value) and a Goon Score for the rangoons (crunch, goo, hot & fresh), from an official reviewer and the community.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
