Goonin Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Goonin is an app for scoring Chinese buffets and their crab rangoons. It is free, shows banner ads, and is an independent project not affiliated with any reviewer, show, YouTube creator, restaurant, Yelp or YouTube.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, password (stored scrambled, we can't read it), username, display name and bio.
  • What you post: reviews, scores, notes, photos, video links, and buffets you add. Reviews, profiles and photos are public: anyone using the app can see them.
  • Your activity: who you follow, buffets you bookmark, accounts you block and reviews you report.
  • Location: only if you allow it. Your location is used on your phone to show nearby buffets. If you tap "use my location" when adding a buffet, the buffet's position is saved; your own location history is not.

This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. We don't sell it.

Google Maps

Buffet ratings, links and "Find buffets near me" use Google Maps. When you search for nearby buffets, the app sends the area you're searching (your approximate location) to our server, which asks Google for Chinese buffets there; your account isn't sent to Google. When you open a buffet, its Google rating is fetched from Google at that moment and isn't stored by us. Google handles these requests under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Delete any review from the app. To delete everything, go to Me → Delete Account: your account, reviews, photos, follows and bookmarks are permanently deleted.

Ads

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, rough location from your IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • iPhone: the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).
  • EU, UK and similar regions: the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and an "Ad privacy choices" link on the Rankings screen lets you change your answer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
